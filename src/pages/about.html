Who we are

Quality products, installed and delivered with care.

Cuzen General Supply Company Limited is a registered limited company, established under the Companies Act. We began with a clear aim: to become a trusted partner in the supply and engineering sector.

Today we distribute office stationery, electrical equipment, construction materials and protective gear, and we provide installation services, including air-conditioning works. We serve Government Ministries, Departments and Agencies through the NEST platform, as well as NGOs and private companies across Tanzania, on both wholesale and retail terms.
