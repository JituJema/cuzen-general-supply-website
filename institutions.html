Ways to buy

Pick the route that matches your rules.

Through NEST

Ministries, Departments and Agencies can buy from us on NEST, Tanzania's national e-procurement system. We follow PPRA guidelines.

By direct quotation

NGOs and companies send us a list. We reply with a quotation covering the supplies and, if needed, the installation.

Wholesale or retail

Bulk quantities for a contract or a few items for one project. Both are welcome.

What we supply to institutions

Five divisions that cover most of your requisitions.

  • Electrical supplies: TBS-approved cables, breakers, boards, lighting
  • Construction materials: cement, steel, aggregates, tools
  • Protective equipment: helmets, boots, gloves, vests, goggles, masks
  • Installation and air-conditioning: wiring, A/C, site installation
